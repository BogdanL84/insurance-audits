# Client-Facing Finding Descriptions

Plain-English explanations for the most common coverage findings.
Use these as the basis for 'plain_english' field in findings — 
write as if explaining to a CFO who is not an insurance professional.

---

## A- the undamaged portion of the building that must be knocked down. B

debris removal expense relating to “A". C - building laws. D - delay in restoring operations.

## Additional Insured – 3rd party Contractual requirement such as customers

correct wording/form needed to avoid breach of Contract.

## Back up of Sewers and Drains

Water or sewage that backs up from a sewer or drain and resulting damage to your property.

## Broader Exclusion by Endorsement

Excess coverage is barred for losses triggered by broader exclusion.

## Claim Prep Fees

Fees to pay an Accounting firm to represent your interests when negotiating with the insurance.

## Coinsurance

A penalty assessed by the carrier in instances where the actual values insured are lower than contractually required.

## Contractual Issues

Your policy must be constructed to function within the contractualy expected framework in an administratively practical way to avoid breach of contract.

## Correct Symbols

we are looking for symbols 1 & 2.

## Coverage Territory

For products sold or that end up outside the coverage territory there is no coverage unless the lawsuit is brought within the coverage territory.

## Custody and Control

Because you have possession of property of others your policy needs to be amended accordingly to provide coverage and or for the proper valuation.

## Drive Other Car

Coverage that amends the BAP to follow the driver.

## Employee as Insured

provides cover to employee when driving their personal vehicle within the scope of their employment .

## Environmental

The current CGL policy does not provide any givebacks relative to the pollution exclusion barring coverage in virtually all scenarios.

## Environmental

You have a significant environmental exposure that would be better covered under a pollution policy covering liability to third parties.

## Errors & Omissions

for losses where there is no bodily injury and or property damage the standard General Liability policy will not respond.

## Flood Triggered Perils

coverage for Ordinance & Law and BI would be barred if flood is insured separately.

## Insured Values

The values insured through the current broker/carrier are insufficient to meet your objectives.

## Leased Employees

When a temporary or leased employees are utilized specific exposures to loss are a potential consequence. If the leased/temporary employee is injured a lawsuit can arise in spite of the worker’s compensation exclusive remedy. This potential liability can and should be transferred to the leasing company.

- Leased Properties -The policy must be constructed to cover your legal liability relative to leased properties

## Waiver of Subrogation

A requirement of many contracts needed to prevent breach of contract. Waiver of Subrogation is a promise from the carrier to forgo legal action against a third party that contributed to a claim when required by written contract.
