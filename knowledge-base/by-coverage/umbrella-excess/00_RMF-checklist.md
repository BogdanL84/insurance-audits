# Umbrella / Excess — Risk Mitigation Factor Checklist

This checklist contains every item to verify when auditing a Umbrella / Excess policy. For each item: find the relevant policy language, note the page number, and assess whether it is Good, Bad, Ugly, or needs verification.

---

## 1. Endorsements, list each one and highlight anything problemat

Endorsements, list each one and highlight anything problematic and reference the applicable page number.

## 2. Exclusions and Non follow form exclusions or endorsements th

Exclusions and Non follow form exclusions or endorsements that are different, unique to the umbrella and/or broader than the underlying. You must note these. At first this RMF seems daunting, but with practice it gets easier.

## 3. 1-Horizontal Limit Exhaustion/Primary and Non-Contributory

1-Horizontal Limit Exhaustion/Primary and Non-Contributory - Does the "other insurance" clause within the condition section of the policy create a primary/non-contributory issue for an Additional Insured named under the primary and umbrella? 2-Transfer of Rights of recovery/Waiver of subrogation follow form.

## 4. Insureds: 1) Is the Additional Insured

Insureds: 1) Is the Additional Insured - follow form, or are conditional exceptions taken? 2) Are named insureds listed the same as in the underlying? Do the named insured after be listed the same as the primary; that is, some umbrella do follow form for. named insureds.

## 5. Limits

Limits - Is Defense inside the limit versus outside the limit? How does your umbrella compare? Typically this PCT will be discovered within E&S policies and not within standard market policies.

## 6. Maintenance of underlying insurance. Must the insured notify

Maintenance of underlying insurance. Must the insured notify the carrier of any underlying changes? Does the insured know this? What is the penalty for not complying - this needs to be defined. If failure to maintain invalidates the umbrella - Houston we have a problem.

## 7. Notice and knowledge. Is the policy endorsed to limit this c

Notice and knowledge. Is the policy endorsed to limit this condition to the person responsible to maintain insurance?

## 8. Per Project Aggregate or Per Location

Per Project Aggregate or Per Location - Do they have these terms within their policy?s Many umbrellas do not have a follow aggregate - which can be problematic when required by contract.

## 9. Professional Liability

Professional Liability - Does the PL endorsement include a means and methods giveback and/or is it broader than the primary?

## 10. Punitive damages, are they excluded?

Punitive damages, are they excluded?

## 11. Right and Duty

Right and Duty - Does the policy state that the carrier has the right and duty to defend when the limits are exhausted in the underlying policy? Typically this PCT will be discovered within E&S policies and not within standard market policies.

## 12. Total Pollution Exclusion

Total Pollution Exclusion - is it endorsed or part of the policy? There are no givebacks under the total pollution exclusion endorsement.

## 13. Underlying Policies

Underlying Policies - Are they all listed, including the foreign liability?

## 14. Specific follow form concerns

Specific follow form concerns - Additional Insured, Named Insured, proffessional
