# Commercial Auto — Risk Mitigation Factor Checklist

This checklist contains every item to verify when auditing a Commercial Auto policy. For each item: find the relevant policy language, note the page number, and assess whether it is Good, Bad, Ugly, or needs verification.

---

## 1. Additional Insured status

Additional Insured status - Automatic? Automatic Waiver of Subrogation? Primary and Non-contributory?

## 2. Bodily injury including Mental Anguish?

Bodily injury including Mental Anguish?

## 3. Broad Named Insured as well as all Named Insureds all listed

Broad Named Insured as well as all Named Insureds all listed

## 4. Lease Gap coverage.

Lease Gap coverage.

## 5. Drive Other Car

Drive Other Car - Including no-fault as applicable? You would like this cover on the broadest basis possible.

## 6. Employee as Insured's CA 9933 & Employee Hired Autos CA 2054

Employee as Insured's CA 9933 & Employee Hired Autos CA 2054. These coverages can be viewed as a positive and a negative - the insured must have this information to make the best decision for them.

## 7. Endorsements

Endorsements - List all endorsements and highlight any that are problematic. Color code problematic endorsements as red with an explanation and include the relevant page number.

## 8. Environmental Exposures

Environmental Exposures - are there any? Does the policy include the limited pollution endorsement as a minimum?

## 9. Fellow Employee Exclusion, is it deleted? Does the insured w

Fellow Employee Exclusion, is it deleted? Does the insured want it deleted? This coverage can be viewed as a positive and a negative - the insured must have this information to make the best decision for them.

## 10. Hired Non-Owned (including liability and PD). Applicable to

Hired Non-Owned (including liability and PD). Applicable to those insureds without an auto exposure such as a condo.

## 11. For vehicles in no-fault states has the insured ever been pr

For vehicles in no-fault states has the insured ever been presented all the optional coverage with the pros and cons?

## 12. Mobile Equipment

Mobile Equipment - What is considered an "auto" and what is considered "mobile equipment"? Are there items not scheduled on the auto that should be?

## 13. Notice and Knowledge.

Notice and Knowledge.

## 14. Ownership of Vehicles

Ownership of Vehicles - Who owns the vehicles? If personally owned or leased - We need a copy of the lease agreement. Also, if personally owned,  the CA 9916 or Employee As Lessor CA 9947 are needed, depending on whether or not the Lessor/Owner is an employee or not.

## 15. Parked Vehicles

Parked Vehicles - Are there a group of vehicles parked close together whereas the Insured would benefit from an aggregate deductible in the event of a catastrophic loss, such as fire or wind that damages more than one vehicle?

## 16. Symbols

Symbols - you are looking for 1 and 2 versus 7. Additionally are there any examples of mobile equipment that are subject to any motor vehicle law that needs to be scheduled? Also, please note that symbols 2,8&9 are not equivalent to symbol 1.

Temporary & Leased Workers? We need to make sure that the definition of an "employee" is modified to exclude "leased" workers.

## 18. Uninsured / Underinsured

Uninsured / Underinsured - Does the Insured understand what they are buying and how much it costs, especially when they have full policy limits? Does the insured understand how their own personal insurance may interact with their commercial Insurance UI or UM limit? This coverage can be viewed as a positive and a negative - the insured must have this information to make the best decision for them.
