# Commercial Property — Risk Mitigation Factor Checklist

This checklist contains every item to verify when auditing a Commercial Property policy. For each item: find the relevant policy language, note the page number, and assess whether it is Good, Bad, Ugly, or needs verification.

---

## 1. Agreed Value

Agreed Value - Does the contract include agreed value wording? Does the policy include a coinsurance provision?

## 2. Back Up of Sewers & Drains, does the policy give coverage ba

Back Up of Sewers & Drains, does the policy give coverage back and  what are the limits?

## 3. BI

BI - Dependent Properties - Does the policy include coverage? What are the limits? Is it suppliers and customers? Are there any natural event exclusions?

## 4. Blanket Coverage

Blanket Coverage - What property exposure are included and/or not included in the blanket? If we can't get full blanket - does a margin clause make sense?

## 5. Business Interruption (BI)

Business Interruption (BI) - what is the BI limit? Is ordinary payroll covered? Does the coverage form include Extra Expense? What is the extended period of indemnity? Is flood covered as a peril to trigger BI? Is ordinance or law covered as a peril to trigger BI? What is the waiting period.

## 6. Claim preparation fees? Are the limits adequate.

Claim preparation fees? Are the limits adequate.

## 7. Contractors Equipment

Contractors Equipment - Does the insured have a floater / Builders Risk / installation floater? Compare terms to BOS?

## 8. Debris Removal: What is the sub-limit and what is the additi

Debris Removal: What is the sub-limit and what is the additional limit?

## 9. Deductibles

Deductibles - Are you paying for something that you are not using?

## 10. Does the insured have Personal Property of others in their c

Does the insured have Personal Property of others in their care custody and control and is the limit and terms adequate? Is the optional valuation for personal property of others triggered? Did you review  property not covered?

## 11. Earthquake

Earthquake - Does the insured have quake coverage? What are the limits? What is the deductible?

## 12. EDP: Coverage including BI? Also, what are the limits for sp

EDP: Coverage including BI? Also, what are the limits for specific EDP cover? The importance of separate EDP increases relative to the insured's dependency upon EDP as relates to their revenue and or profit.

## 13. Employee Dishonesty/Forgery or alteration /Social Engineerin

Employee Dishonesty/Forgery or alteration /Social Engineering/Fraudulent Transfer of Funds/Third party Crime. If under a separate policy, just indicate so and the review can be separate. Often some crime coverage can be found under the property policy.

## 14. Endorsements

Endorsements - List all endorsements and highlight any that are problematic. Color code problematic endorsements as red with an explanation and include the relevant page number.

## 15. Voluntary Parting and unauthorized Instructions.

Voluntary Parting and unauthorized Instructions.

## 16. Flood including BI

Flood including BI - Do they have coverage? What are the limits? What is the deductible? If flood is provided by the NFIP then BI is not covered under the property policy when caused by flood.

## 17. Insuring to Value

Insuring to Value - What is the replacement cost per sq. foot? What is Marshal and Swift calculation?  How did the client calculate their Business personal property? 🔍🔦

## 18. Inventory

Inventory - Do inventory values fluctuate? Do we need a reporting form or peak season endorsement?

## 19. Mechanical Breakdown Coverage

Mechanical Breakdown Coverage - Is MB included, what are the terms?

## 20. Miscellaneous Unnamed Location (off premises property)- If c

Miscellaneous Unnamed Location (off premises property)- If covered, what are the limits? What perils are covered, what perils can we add, what does the lease require if applicable? Are there any restrictions to this coverage? For flood and quake perils can we add, with some limitations on what zones are acceptable? For organization where newly aquired is important, ask them, make sure these limits are as high as practical/possible.

## 21. Ocean Cargo

Ocean Cargo - Does the insured  ship overseas or outside the coverage territory?🔍🔦

## 22. Off Premises Power Interruption

Off Premises Power Interruption - Is  direct and indirect coverage provided. What are the sub-limits? Are overhead transmission lines covered? Are the mechanical breakdown exposures covered?

## 23. Ordinance or law

Ordinance or law - Does the prospect have all 4 coverages and are the limits adequate? If flood is insured under NFIP, then O&L caused by flood is not covered under the property policy.

## 24. Pollution Clean Up

Pollution Clean Up - are the limits sufficient for the exposure?🔍🔦 Does the insured need an environmental clean up policy?

## 25. Coverage modifications: 1-Is there property Not Covered

Coverage modifications: 1-Is there property Not Covered - that should be covered? 2-Are improvements and betterments covered for the correct value at leased locations? 3 - Does the policy have a temperature and humidity exclusion and can we modify it?

## 26. Protective Safeguards

Protective Safeguards - is there a protective safeguard endorsement included, what are the safeguards listed and does the insured have the safeguards as well as being aware of the safeguard conditions?

## 27. Spoilage / Consequential

Spoilage / Consequential - Does the insured have the cover/exposure and if so what are the terms/limits? These exposures can be hard to think of - use your imagination.

## 28. Time Element Deductible

Time Element Deductible - Are there any and what are they and can they be reduced?

## 29. Transit

Transit - Is the limit adequate? How is property being shipped? Is contingent liability included? Do we need a separate policy. Is the coverage broad? Are all conveyances included?

## 30. Vacancy Restrictions

Vacancy Restrictions - What are terms? Could the insured have a vacancy? Does the insured know what the restrictions are? Do we need a vacancy permit.

## 31. Waiver of subrogation

Waiver of subrogation - see General tab. For proprety this is often built in
