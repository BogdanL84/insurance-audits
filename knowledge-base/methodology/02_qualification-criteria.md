# Qualification Criteria — 18 Questions

Complete this form in your car immediately after the first meeting. Failure to properly qualify is the #1 reason brokers don't write business.

1. Do you know who the current agent is and for how long?
2. Do you know who the current carrier is and for how long?
3. What is the relationship with the agent?
4. Do you know who the decision makers are?
5. Will you get to present to ALL the decision makers?
6. What is their decision making criteria and process?
7. When was the last time they went out to market and what happened? (History repeats itself)
8. Did the incumbent get a second look the last time they marketed? If yes, does that mean the client overpays when no one else is competitive?
9. Why are they marketing this year?
10. Are there any other agents involved? What are the relationships?
11. Have you discussed picking the best broker and carrier?
12. Is the prospect fully qualified?
13. Would you get the account if your price was the same?
14. Will the incumbent have an opportunity to address the same pains? (Three possible incumbent responses: didn't know = bad; can fix it now = bad; downplays it = bad)
15. What is the estimated revenue?
16. What have we not thought of?
17. Do you have an upfront contract with the prospect? If you do A, B & C, they will hire you?
18. Have you played it forward? Are you preparing for the actions of the other broker — desperate brokers do desperate things!
