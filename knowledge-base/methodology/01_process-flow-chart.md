# TBV Process Flow Chart — Step by Step

## Step 1: First Meeting
- Get prospect thinking differently / initial qualification
- Procure documents and upfront contract
- Complete qualification form in car immediately after meeting
- Start strategy form in car — imagine how you will be successful
- Debrief using Challenger Sale debrief questions with mentor

## Step 2: Pre-Conference Call Analysis
- Complete analysis form with focus on developing questions for the call

## Step 3: Conference Call
- Merge strategic questions with pre-conference call analysis questions
- Read questions out loud — make sure you are comfortable with them
- Conduct call and record (with consent)
- Use baton method for multiple callers — only the person with the baton speaks

## Step 4: Post-Conference Call Analysis
- Read conference call transcript carefully
- Reconcile pre-conference call analysis against what you learned
- "Mona Lisa this step" — perfect it but don't go beyond diminishing returns

## Step 5: Progress Report
- Confirm final meeting (do you go first or second?)
- Discuss 1-2 items with buyer to gauge reaction — dress rehearsal for final meeting
- Address additional qualification questions if needed

## Step 6: Prepare for Final Meeting
- Finish strategy form and brainstorm with teammates
- Pick 10 PCTs (Poorly Constructed Terms) for presentation
- Complete "Connecting with Your Customer" for each PCT
- Prepare Executive Summary with all 10 PCTs (may only use 3-5 but be prepared)
- Organize and mark up documents in 3-ring binder(s)

## Step 7: Final Meeting
- BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) — lead with price
- Set up docs: Broker A on left, Broker B on right
- Final qualification — confirm excitement or nervousness
- Present using Executive Summary as exhibit, slow down and trial close each PCT
- Have Broker A vs Broker B conversation after first or second PCT
- Close — flush out objections, understand timeline
- Play it forward and re-close
