# Winning the Account — Final Meeting Strategy Framework

## 1. Analyze Your Customers and Stakeholders
- Identify decision makers and stakeholders
- What do clients want (stated) vs need (unstated)?
- Do we have a "big bomb" — one finding so compelling it wins the account alone?

## 2. Analyze Your Competitors
- How do we compare to the competition? (Be critical and honest)
- What can the competition bring that we can't?
- Do we need to play defense on anything?

## 3. Brainstorm Options
- Which PCTs, ideas, and areas of discussion to use for THIS client?
- What are their hot buttons — WANTED and NEEDED?
- At this stage, every idea is a good idea.

## 4. Examine Opportunities and Threats
- What can go wrong? Decision maker doesn't show up? We get rolled? They take comments defensively? They want to discuss with their broker?
- What can you do about each threat?

## 5. Solve Problems
- What are our solutions?
- Always quantify the financial benefit — never present a problem without a number
- Solutions = policy change AND/OR risk management concepts

## 6. Evaluate Options
- Choose the best PCTs and establish presentation order
- Build the case logically and intuitively
- Always lead with price (BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front)

## 7. Connecting with Your Customer (for each PCT)
For every PCT you present, prepare:
1. Plain English explanation (8th grade level)
2. Specific recommendation to fix it
3. Evidence foundation — what did the client say or what did you find?
4. How to get Attention and Interest (motive)
5. Conviction evidence — testimonial, case study, policy wording, court case
6. Benefit to the client — what's in it for them?
7. Financial quantification — you MUST put a number on it
8. Analogy or story to make it stick
9. Trial close to use
10. How to handle objections
11. Play it forward — what does the client do next?
