# The Messina Method — Sales Presentation Framework

Use this for first meeting scripts and Strategic Advisor output.

## Step 1: Motive
Open: "Why do we buy insurance?" — pause, let them answer.
Three motives: (1) Law requires it. (2) Contracts require it. (3) Protect assets.

## Step 2: Insurance Has a Bad Name — Because It's Intangible
Choose 2-3 analogies:
- **Briefcase:** $2M locked inside, don't get keys till the claim
- **Turkey:** Safe every day until Thanksgiving week
- **Pillow:** 7 minutes tells you if it works; 7 years with a policy might not
- **Car Seat:** Best one fails if fitted wrong — same with insurance
- **Challenger:** False sense of security from repeated success

## Step 3: Remedy — Move the Future to Today
Preempt objections first: "My broker does this." / "Never had an uncovered claim." / "Reputable firm."
Reframe: "The past doesn't dictate the future. The future dictates the past."
Broker A (transaction only) vs Broker B (verifies). "Trust but verify."

## Step 4: The Process — The Einstein Approach
"If I had only one hour to save the world, I would spend 55 minutes defining the problem and 5 minutes finding the solution."
"We invest 40 hours. It shows where you're overpaying and where you're uncovered. If you benefit, we benefit."

## Step 5: Close
"We eliminate the false sense of security. No crystal ball — but we bring tomorrow's risks into today."
Final line: "If you benefit, we benefit. That's the partnership I want to build."
