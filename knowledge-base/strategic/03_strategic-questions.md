# Strategic Questions — Seeking to Understand (Conference Call)

Combine these with questions developed from your pre-conference call analysis before the call.

## Big Picture Questions
1. How was your company started and how did you grow to where you are today?
2. How is your company doing in the current marketplace?
3. What are your company's top three goals over the next 3-5 years?
4. What are your top 3 barriers to achieving those goals?
5. What benefits do you derive if your company succeeds?
6. Do you believe you are overpaying for insurance? (Ask toward end of meeting)
7. What keeps you up at night regarding insurance?
8. How would you rank: compliance, conducting business, protecting assets? (They can be equal)
9. Walk us through your operations from start to finish.

## Workers Comp / MOD Questions
1. Do you know what your Mod is?
2. Are you aware how your mod is calculated?
3. What would you like your mod to be? ("Any objective for your future mod?")
4. What do you do to lower your mod?
5. Have you ever had a change to your mod after it was released? (Is a mod review being done?)
6. Do you have a drug testing program?
7. How do you prepare for an audit?
8. How do you determine employee classification?
9. What states did you perform work in last year? (Automatic coverage does not extend to new states)

## Loss Prevention / Claims Questions
1. How do you manage claims? ("Tell me what happens when a claim occurs?")
2. What do you do when a comp claim happens? (Return to work program, accident investigator, weekly calls to injured employee)
3. What are your 3 largest risk factors contributing to claims?
4. What has been your claim experience?
5. What do you do when you have an auto accident? (Are there packets in the glove box?)
6. Do you have any kind of formal claims review? Who attends?
7. Do you run annual MVRs for employees? Any rules regarding violations?
8. Are there consequences related to safety? Good performance rewarded?
9. Do you have a fleet safety program?
10. Do you have a return to work program?
11. When an employee is injured, how do they decide what doctor to go to? (Are they using a network?)
12. Is safety part of supervisor reviews? How is it measured?
