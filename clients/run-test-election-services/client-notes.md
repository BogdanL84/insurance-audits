## Notes
Run-Test Election Services specializes in providing election technology solutions, including printing, mailing, and processing services that enhance the accuracy, efficiency, and security of elections. They serve local, county, and state election officials across 31 states and the District of Columbia, impacting over 90 million registered voters in the United States. With over 50 years of experience in the election industry, their focus is on instilling voter confidence through cutting-edge technology and dependable service. The company's solutions encompass a wide range of services, including ballot printing, election mail sorting, and emergency support technology.
